Who we are

I AM Realty & Management (“we,” “us”) is a real estate business led by Cynthia Allen, a California real estate licensee (CA DRE #[DRE NUMBER]), affiliated with [BROKERAGE NAME]. This policy explains how we handle information collected through this website.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our forms, call or email us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • What you’re looking for (for example, buying, selling, renting or a valuation)
  • Property details, such as property type or address
  • Anything else you choose to include in your message

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services that load our fonts may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you visit. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To provide real estate services, such as home valuations, showings and transaction support
  • To schedule appointments and keep you updated on your purchase, sale or property
  • To keep our website running securely and block spam
  • To meet legal, licensing and record-keeping requirements

How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to serve you:

  • Service providers who help us run this website, deliver form submissions and send email
  • Parties to your transaction, such as lenders, escrow, title, inspectors or other agents, when you ask us to or it’s needed to complete your transaction
  • Our brokerage, as required for licensing and supervision
  • Legal authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights

“Do Not Track” signals

Our website does not track visitors over time or across other websites, so it does not change its behavior in response to “Do Not Track” browser signals. Third parties do not collect information about your online activities over time and across websites through our site.

Your choices

You can ask us to review, correct or delete the personal information you’ve shared with us, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Email Cynthia@IAmRealty.net or call (323) 481-0889. Some records may need to be kept to meet legal or licensing requirements.

How we protect and keep your information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, and we keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Links to other websites

Our site may link to other websites, such as property listings or lender tools. We aren’t responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top of this page.
